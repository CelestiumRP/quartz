Celestium's land claiming system is based on food. Food will be the upkeep of your land, and will prevent people from placing blocks inside of your area.

## How to make a Stronghold or City
Creating a stronghold or city is fairly easy, however,  you will need to have textiles to be able to start the process. You can create either a Stronghold or City flag. The Stronghold flag will protect a 64x64x64 around the flag, meaning that only people in the league can place blocks, however, anyone can break the blocks at any time, so it is best to keep things reinforced. City flags work the same way, but have a protection radius of 96x96x96 instead. Once you place a flag, you must put a piece of cloth on it, and raise it, before you do, ensure you have some source of food directly next to the flag, this can be a chest, trunk, or any other container, make sure it has food. Then, right click to raise the flag.

You can rename the Stronghold or City by using the command /stronghold name < name >.


**Stronghold/City Upkeep**

In order to keep your stronghold active. you will need to have food,  these can be provided by logic posts, simply place a logic post in a cellar, attach a flag to it, and ensure there are containers around it, these will supply food for your stronghold.

1 Satiety of food allows a stronghold/city to stay alive for .001 days, this is variable based on siege intensity, which is changed by killing players, breaking blocks, and other means.


## Inviting Player Groups

Once you make your Stronghold or City, you will need to invite player groups to be able to modify, place, or interact with anything in the protected area. You can do this via /stronghold league < groupname >, and remove them with /stronghold stopleague < groupname >
